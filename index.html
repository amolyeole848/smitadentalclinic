Our Services

Complete Dental Care in Nashik

Transparent pricing, expert care, and modern treatment for every dental need.

🔬
Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

Root canal treatment removes infected pulp to save your natural tooth. Using modern rotary endodontic techniques, Dr. Smita completes most RCTs in just 1–2 comfortable sittings. Digital X-rays ensure precision diagnosis and comfortable treatment.

✅ Saves natural tooth ✅ Completed in 1–2 sittings ✅ Painless procedure ✅ Long-lasting results
₹2,500 – ₹6,000 per tooth
✨
Teeth Cleaning (Scaling & Polishing)

Professional scaling removes hardened tartar and plaque that regular brushing cannot. Polishing leaves your teeth feeling smooth and looking brighter. Recommended every 6 months for optimal gum health and fresh breath.

✅ Removes plaque & tartar ✅ Prevents gum disease ✅ Freshens breath ✅ Brightens smile
₹800 – ₹1,500
🦷
Dental Implants

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. A titanium post is surgically placed into your jawbone and topped with a natural-looking crown. Implants look, feel, and function exactly like real teeth — and last a lifetime with proper care.

✅ Permanent replacement ✅ Natural look & feel ✅ Preserves jawbone ✅ Lifetime durability
₹18,000 – ₹35,000 per implant
💎
Cosmetic Dentistry

Transform your smile with our cosmetic treatments including teeth whitening, dental veneers, composite bonding, and complete smile design. Whether you want a subtle refresh or a complete makeover, Dr. Smita creates personalised smile plans tailored to your face and goals.

✅ Teeth whitening ✅ Porcelain veneers ✅ Smile design ✅ Composite bonding
₹1,500 – ₹25,000 (treatment-dependent)
🩺
Tooth Extraction

When a tooth cannot be saved, gentle extraction is performed under local anaesthesia ensuring maximum comfort. We also handle complex wisdom tooth extractions with care and provide complete post-extraction aftercare instructions for quick healing.

✅ Painless procedure ✅ Wisdom teeth specialist ✅ Fast healing ✅ Full aftercare support
₹500 – ₹3,000
